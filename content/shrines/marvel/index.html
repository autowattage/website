marvel

when i say marvel i really just mean I LIKE SPIDERMAN!!!

i wasn't too personally interested in marvel growing up, but when i watched "into the spiderverse" i had a hyperfixation that lasted for days. then i forgot about it until i watched "into the spiderverse" and was fixated for a few days. and then i forgot about spiderman.

the real 'fixation' starts during my gap year. me and a few of my friends were going to watch the new 'brand new day' movie, but i had no idea what it was all about. i actually thought it was another one from the animated spiderverse series, and i watched the trailer in the uber LOL.

i was so fascinated with brand new day after watching it that i ended up watching it again!! and then a third time to get an exclusive poster (and got ice cream after)!! for the weeks following, i'd frequent acon, olive, and amber's shared apartment to binge watch spiderman movies together. we watched all of the tom holland movies, then watched ironman 1 and 2. i had so much fun!!!

here's my spidersona! it's just bunnyguy but more spider-like. me and acon drew our spidersonas together the day of watching bdn for the first time.
